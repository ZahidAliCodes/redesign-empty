Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services and process

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. Simple websites take 4-6 weeks, while complex applications can take 3-6 months. We provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes! We offer 24/7 support, maintenance, and monitoring services. All projects include a warranty period, and we provide long-term support packages.

What security measures do you implement?

Security is our top priority. We follow secure coding practices, conduct security audits, implement encryption, and ensure compliance with industry standards like GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC2.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Absolutely! We specialize in system integration and can work with your existing infrastructure, APIs, and databases to ensure seamless connectivity.

Do you offer fixed-price projects?

We offer both fixed-price and hourly billing options. For well-defined projects, we provide detailed fixed-price quotes. For ongoing development, hourly billing offers more flexibility.

What technologies do you specialize in?

We work with modern technologies including React, Node.js, Python, Java, .NET, AWS, Azure, mobile development (iOS/Android), AI/ML, and blockchain solutions.

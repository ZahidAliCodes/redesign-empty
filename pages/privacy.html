Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data protection are our top priorities. Learn how we safeguard your information.

Information We Collect

At iDigital, we collect information that you provide directly to us, such as when you:

  • Contact us for services or support
  • Fill out forms on our website
  • Subscribe to our newsletter
  • Request quotes or consultations

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Send you updates about our services (with your consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect against fraud and unauthorized access

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes:

  • SSL encryption for all data transmissions
  • Regular security audits and assessments
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Compliance with industry standards (SOC 2, ISO 27001)

Government Compliance

As a GSA approved contractor with DOD active approval, we maintain the highest standards of data protection and comply with:

  • Federal Information Security Management Act (FISMA)
  • Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS)
  • Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC)
  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to help us operate our business. These service providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the services they provide to us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of communications
  • Data portability
